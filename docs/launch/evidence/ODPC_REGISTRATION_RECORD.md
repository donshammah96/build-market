## 1. Statutory Registration Threshold Pre-Check

Under Regulation 13 and the Third Schedule of the _Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021_, entities operating in Kenya must assess their mandatory registration obligations based on two criteria:

### Criterion A: General Commercial Thresholds

- **Annual Turnover:** Exceeding KES 5,000,000.
- **Employee Count:** Exceeding 10 employees.
- _Current MVP Status:_ Build Market operates as an early-stage venture under these numerical thresholds.

### Criterion B: Mandatory Sectors & Sensitive Processing (Regulation 13(4))

Entities processing sensitive personal data or operating in specific high-impact economic activities are **mandated to register regardless of turnover or employee count**, including:

1. Processing of sensitive personal data (e.g., national identity cards, biometric data, financial information).
2. Operating digital marketplace matchmaking connecting commercial contractors with the public.
3. Conducting statutory credential verification across national licensing databases.

## 2. Legal Determination & Decision Record

- **Determination:** Build Market Technologies Ltd **qualifies for mandatory statutory registration** with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) as both a **Data Controller** (determining the purposes of homeowner matchmaking and trust verification) and a **Data Processor** (processing payments and verification data on behalf of commercial contractors).
- **Registration Class:** Medium Data Controller / Processor under Third Schedule.
- **Registration Portal:** [dataportal.odpc.go.ke](https://dataportal.odpc.go.ke)
